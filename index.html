📋 How This Activity Works – Three Stages

Stage 1: Individual Work ⏳ 30 minutes

Complete the templates below for your chosen business. Fill in:

  • Business Information – name, description, why you chose it
  • PESTLE Analysis – all 6 external factors with sources
  • SWOT Analysis – strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats
  • TOWS Matrix – SO, WO, ST, WT strategies
  • Recommendations – 3-5 clear, actionable recommendations
Stage 2: Peer Feedback ⏳ 15 minutes

Find a partner. Swap your work (share your screen, email, or printed copy). Then:

  • Read your partner's analysis carefully
  • Complete the Peer Feedback section below for your partner
  • Be specific – point out strengths, gaps, and suggestions
  • Be kind – this is about helping, not judging
Stage 3: Class Sharing ⏳ 15 minutes

Your tutor will facilitate a class discussion. Be ready to share:

  • The best feedback you received – what was most helpful?
  • One thing you will change based on the feedback
  • One insight you gained from giving feedback to someone else
